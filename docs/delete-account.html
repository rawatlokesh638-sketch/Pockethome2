🛡️ User Rights & App Store Compliance

Account & User Data Deletion

PocketHome complies fully with Indus Appstore (by PhonePe), Google Play Developer Policies, and Indian Personal Data Protection standards. You have absolute ownership and control over your data.

📲 Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

If you currently have the PocketHome app installed on your Android device:

  1. Open PocketHome on your device.
  2. On the Home Screen, tap your Profile Avatar in the top-right header or scroll down to the bottom footer.
  3. Tap the red button labeled "Delete Account & All Data" (or "Delete Account & Wipe All Data").
  4. Read the warning prompt and tap "Delete Permanently (हटाएं)" to confirm.
Instant Execution: All your expenses, incomes, recurring bills, budget targets, chat logs, and account credentials are permanently wiped immediately from our servers and your local storage.

Zero-Retention Policy: We do not retain backup copies or archived databases of deleted accounts. When an account deletion is initiated, data is permanently expunged. Email requests are executed within 24 to 48 hours of receipt.
